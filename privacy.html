Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how From Minnow to Monarch handles information when you use the app. Contact us at hengbozail@163.com with privacy questions.

Local Game Data

The app stores gameplay progress, unlocked levels, star results, tutorial state, audio preferences, and advertising milestone flags locally on your device. This data is used to operate the game and is not used by us to identify you.

Advertising and Tracking Permission

The app includes third-party advertising technology. After the home screen is visible, the app may ask for permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you allow tracking, advertising partners may use the device advertising identifier and related information to provide or measure advertising. If you deny permission, the app continues to function and the advertising partners receive the permission status instead of a tracking identifier.

The app may initialize advertising services from Chartboost, Yandex Mobile Ads, Vungle, and InMobi. These providers may process information under their own privacy policies when their services are available. Advertising is only attempted after the first successful completion of designated milestone levels; it is not required to play the game.

Security Service

The app initializes the bundled HSSecurity service on physical devices after the tracking-permission step. This service supports security and fraud-prevention functions. It may process device-related information as needed for those functions.

Audio and Device Access

Music and sound-effect choices are saved locally. The app does not request access to your photos, contacts, microphone, or precise location for normal gameplay.

Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can address the request.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its services change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
