Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 19, 2026

Neon Exit Puzzle is provided by Edy. This policy explains how the app handles information when you play.

Information stored on your device

The game stores progress and preferences locally on your device. This may include unlocked levels, stars, best move counts, best times, an in-progress puzzle, audio, haptic and reduced-motion settings, and advertising milestone status. This local information is used to operate the game.

Advertising and tracking permission

The app may request permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework. If you allow tracking, the app may access the advertising identifier (IDFA) for advertising-related purposes. If you decline, the game remains playable.

The app retrieves advertising configuration from its service and may initialize advertising partners only when valid configuration is available. These partners may include Chartboost, Yandex Mobile Ads, Vungle, and InMobi. They may process information under their own privacy policies to provide, measure, or personalize advertising where permitted by law and your device settings.

Security service

On physical devices, the app initializes a security service using the app’s bundle identifier and the device’s vendor identifier (IDFV). This helps protect the app and advertising-related services from fraud and misuse. The service is not initialized in the iOS Simulator.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored game progress and settings remain on your device until you delete the app or clear its data through device settings. Advertising partners handle information they receive under their own policies and retention practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The current version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
