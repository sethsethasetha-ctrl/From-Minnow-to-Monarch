An Offline Ocean Growth Game

From Minnow to Monarch

Read the tide, outswim predators, and grow from a tiny fish into the ruler of a luminous underwater world.

Grow with every bite

Steer with a responsive fixed joystick, collect plankton, and eat fish at your tier or below. Evolve from Minnow to Dart, Hunter, Apex, and finally Monarch before time runs out.

Ride the Current

Changing tides speed you up or slow you down. Use them to reach food and escape danger.

Make the Dash Count

Spend rechargeable Dash energy for a short burst, or conserve it while hiding in a safety vortex.

Master 30 Levels

Unlock six ocean regions, earn stars, and replay deterministic challenges to improve your best run.
